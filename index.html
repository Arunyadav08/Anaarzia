OUR STORY

Born from a Love for Pets

PawStore was founded in 2020 by a group of pet lovers in Bengaluru who were frustrated by the lack of quality, affordable pet products in India. We believed every pet deserved the very best — regardless of budget.

Today we serve thousands of happy pet parents across 500+ cities in India, offering vet-approved, premium-quality products delivered right to your doorstep at honest prices.
